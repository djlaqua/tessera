# Market and Competition

All competitor and market statements are `VERIFY` until sourced in `08-research-backlog.md`.

## Option comparison (assessment)
| Criterion | A: Localization | B: Middleware | Hybrid |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Time to first revenue | 12 to 24+ months | 3 to 9 months | 3 to 9 months |
| Early cost | $2k to $15k+ | $0 to $500 | $0 to $500, then hardware |
| Competition | Heavy: defense primes, funded navigation and autonomy startups | Moderate to heavy in defense (TAK ecosystem, integrators), lighter in civil niches | Differentiated by combination |
| Moat | Deep (IP, data, validation) | Lock-in, protocol depth | Both |
| Solo feasibility | Hard past simulation | High | High, staged |

## Recommendation
Hybrid is correct, with a strict order: **B first on a narrow wedge, then A as integrity
scoring only**. Full visual navigation competes head on with well funded players. An
integrity or confidence layer that sits beside any navigation stack is a smaller, less
contested problem and reuses the replay harness.

## Landscape to research (VERIFY)
- TAK ecosystem (ATAK, TAK Server, open-source servers) and its plugin market.
- Autopilot ecosystems exposing telemetry (MAVLink, PX4, ArduPilot vendors).
- Command and control and COP vendors, including European ones.
- Visual and terrain-aided navigation vendors and academic baselines.
- Generic IoT and GIS integration platforms.

## Gaps to test in discovery
1. Operators with mixed fleets who lose data on weak links.
2. Post-incident replay and audit needs (insurance, regulators).
3. Demand for a trust score on position from any source.
