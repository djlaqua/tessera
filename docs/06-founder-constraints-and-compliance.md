# Founder Constraints and Compliance

Everything here is `VERIFY` with a qualified German advisor. This is a planning note, not legal advice.

## Constraints from the founder doc
- Operating from Germany with limited German, strong English.
- Personal legal and residency details are kept out of this public repo (use the gitignored `private/` folder).
- Phase 1 stays unclassified, civil and dual-use.

## Checklist
- [ ] Confirm your residence status allows self-employment or company founding (local immigration office).
- [ ] Review employer contract for IP assignment and side business clauses before any code is written on company time or hardware.
- [ ] Keep personal and employer work strictly separate (devices, accounts, repos).
- [ ] Check EU dual-use regulation and BAFA scope for the Phase 2 localization module before sharing code or demos outside the EU.
- [ ] Decide entity (UG vs GmbH) after the above, with a tax advisor.
- [ ] Review open-source license choice against dual-use and export implications.
- [ ] Confirm NATO DIANA and EU program eligibility rules directly from program pages.

## Language plan
Build, sell, and document in English. Add a German-speaking commercial advisor or co-founder before targeting Behörden, Vergaberecht tenders, or German-language grants (EXIST, ZIM).

## Operational security
Treat location data, customer telemetry, and demo flight logs as sensitive. Use synthetic or consented data in public demos.
